Chapter 01

Anatomy

Simpler than it looks.

Pull it apart and there’s a plastic core holding 26 visible pieces. Each kind of piece follows its own rules.

6 centres

Fixed to the core, they only spin in place. So a centre’s colour is its face’s colour. White is always opposite yellow.

12 edges

Two stickers each, always sitting between two centres. An edge can be in the right place and still be flipped.

8 corners

Three stickers each. A corner can be in its home and still be twisted one of three ways.

Pieces, not colours.

Solving means putting each piece back where it belongs without undoing the ones you’ve already placed.

Chapter 02

Notation

Every move is a letter.

Each face is named for where it sits: Up, Down, Left, Right, Front and Back.

R = a quarter turn, clockwise

A letter on its own turns that face clockwise, as if you were looking straight at it. R lifts the right face up and away from you.

R′ backwards · R2 twice

A prime mark turns it counter-clockwise; a 2 is a half turn. An algorithm is just a sentence written in these letters.

Your turn.

Tap a move, or type U D L R F B on your keyboard (hold Shift for prime).
