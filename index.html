01 — Anatomy

Twenty-six pieces.
Three kinds.

Take it apart and it’s simpler than it looks. A plastic core holds 26 visible pieces, and each kind moves by its own rules.

6 centres. Fixed to the core, they only ever spin in place, so a centre’s colour is its face’s colour. White always sits opposite yellow.

12 edges. Two stickers each, always between two centres. An edge can be in the right place and still be flipped.

8 corners. Three stickers each. A corner can sit in its home yet be twisted one of three ways.

Solving isn’t about colours. It’s about putting each piece back where it belongs, without undoing the ones you’ve already placed.

02 — Notation

Every move is
a letter.

Each face is named for where it sits: Up, Down, Left, Right, Front and Back.

A letter on its own is a quarter turn clockwise, as if you were looking straight at that face. R turns the right face up and away from you.

Add a prime, R′, to turn counter-clockwise, or a 2, R2, for a half turn. An algorithm is just a sentence written in these letters.

Your turn. Tap a move, or type U D L R F B (add Shift for prime).

03 — Methods

Three ways home.
